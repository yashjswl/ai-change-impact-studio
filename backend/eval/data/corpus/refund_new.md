# New Process: Tiered Automated Refunds

## Overview
Customers request refunds in the self-service Help Center. Low-value requests from
customers in good standing are approved automatically, and agents spend their time
on the larger or riskier requests. Payouts run daily instead of weekly.

## Steps
1. The customer opens the Help Center, selects the order and chooses a reason code
   from a fixed list. Photos can be attached for damaged items.
2. The system checks the refund window and the customer's refund history.
3. A refund under $50 is approved instantly if the customer's refund rate over the
   past 12 months is below 10% and the order was delivered within 30 days.
4. A refund from $50 to $300 is routed to a support agent, who must respond within
   a 4 hour service level.
5. A refund above $300 needs review by both the support manager and a fraud analyst.
6. Approved refunds are paid in a daily run and reach the customer within
   2 business days.

## Customer Options
Customers can choose store credit instead of a payment to the original method.
Store credit carries a 10% bonus. The refund window has been extended from 14 days
to 30 days after delivery.

## Fraud Controls
A fraud scoring model rates every request. Requests with a score above 0.8 are sent
for manual review even when they fall under the automatic limit. The system flags a
customer automatically after 3 refunds within 60 days, and the flag is visible to
every agent.

## Reporting
Reason codes are captured for every request. A weekly product-quality report shows
which items generate the most refunds, so merchandising can act on the data.

## Expected Outcomes
- Average time from request to money back falls from 9 days to under 2 days.
- Most small refunds need no agent time, which frees capacity for complex cases.
- Fraud review becomes systematic instead of relying on 1 in 20 manual spot checks.
