# Old Process: Manual Customer Refund Approval

## Overview
Refund requests are handled one at a time by support agents through email. Approval
depends on the size of the refund, and Finance pays refunds in a weekly batch. The
process has no self-service option for customers.

## Steps
1. The customer emails customer support to request a refund.
2. The agent looks up the order in the order system and replies to ask for photo
   proof if the customer reports a damaged item.
3. The agent records the request in the Refund Request Sheet, a shared Google
   Sheet, and checks that the request falls inside the refund window.
4. A refund above $20 needs approval from a team lead, who is asked through a chat
   message. A refund above $100 also needs the support manager.
5. Approved requests wait in the sheet until Finance processes them in a weekly
   batch every Thursday.
6. Finance pays the refund to the original payment method only. Store credit is
   not offered.
7. The agent emails the customer to confirm that the refund has been issued.

## Policy
Refunds are accepted within 14 days of delivery. Requests after that date are
declined unless a manager makes an exception, and there is no record of how often
exceptions are granted.

## Fraud Controls
Fraud checks are limited to manual spot checks on 1 in 20 refunds. Agents rely on
memory to recognise customers who request refunds repeatedly. There is no shared
list of repeat refund requesters.

## Known Issues
- Customers wait 9 days on average from request to money back.
- Approvals by chat message are slow, and requests are sometimes forgotten when a
  team lead is out of the office.
- Refund reasons are written as free text, so the company cannot report which
  products cause the most refunds.
- Agents spend a large part of each day on repetitive small refunds.
