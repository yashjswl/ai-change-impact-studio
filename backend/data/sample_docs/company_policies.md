# Relevant Company Policies

## Data Retention & Privacy
All employee personal data (including SSN, bank details, and tax forms)
must be stored only in approved, access-controlled systems. Physical
storage of documents containing SSN or bank details is discouraged and must
be phased out by end of fiscal year. Digital forms must use e-signature
tools on the approved vendor list and must encrypt data at rest.

## System Access Provisioning
All system access must be granted according to role-based access control
(RBAC) templates approved by IT Security. Access grants outside the
standard template require a documented exception approved by the employee's
manager and IT Security.

## Compliance Training
All new hires must complete mandatory compliance training (code of conduct,
information security, anti-harassment) within 30 days of their start date.
Completion must be tracked and reportable for audit purposes. Managers are
responsible for ensuring their reports complete required training on time.

## Change Management
Any change to a core HR, IT, or Finance process that affects more than one
department must go through the Change Advisory Board (CAB) for review, and
must include a stakeholder communication plan and a rollback plan before
go-live.
