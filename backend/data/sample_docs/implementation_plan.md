# Implementation Plan: Automated Onboarding Rollout

## Timeline
- **Weeks 1-2:** Configure workflow engine, ATS/HRIS integration, and IT
  provisioning ticket templates in a sandbox environment.
- **Weeks 3-4:** Pilot with one department (Engineering), collect feedback,
  fix integration issues.
- **Week 5:** Change Advisory Board review and sign-off.
- **Week 6:** Company-wide rollout with all new hires processed through the
  new workflow. Old manual process retired.
- **Weeks 7-8:** Hypercare period, HR and IT staff on standby for
  escalations; daily status review.

## Owners
- HR Operations: workflow configuration, digital forms, LMS integration.
- IT: provisioning ticket templates, RBAC template mapping, laptop
  logistics.
- Finance: payroll integration testing.
- Project Sponsor (VP People): CAB submission, executive communication.

## Risks Identified
- Integration between ATS and HRIS may have data mapping errors for
  non-standard employment types (contractors, interns).
- Managers may bypass the new workflow and revert to emailing HR directly
  out of habit.
- IT provisioning SLAs assume RBAC templates cover all roles; new or
  unusual roles may still require manual exception handling.

## Training Plan
- HR Operations: hands-on workflow engine training, 2 sessions, week 2.
- IT: provisioning ticket template training, 1 session, week 2.
- Hiring Managers: 30-minute walkthrough of new ATS "Accepted" trigger and
  what to expect, delivered before pilot (week 3).
