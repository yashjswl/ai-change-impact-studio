# Old Process: Manual Employee Onboarding

## Overview
When a new employee is hired, the hiring manager emails HR a PDF offer
summary. HR manually creates a paper onboarding folder and schedules a
first-day meeting.

## Steps
1. Hiring manager emails HR with new hire name, start date, and role.
2. HR manually enters the employee into the HRIS system by hand (typically
   1-2 business days after the email).
3. HR prints and mails a paper welcome packet, tax forms (W-4, I-9), and
   benefits enrollment forms to the employee's home address.
4. IT is notified via a separate email from HR, sent after the HRIS entry is
   complete, to provision a laptop, email account, and system access.
   Provisioning typically takes 3-5 business days and is done manually by an
   IT technician who follows a printed checklist.
5. The employee completes paper tax and benefits forms and returns them by
   mail or in person on day one.
6. HR manually files the paper forms in a physical cabinet and re-keys
   selected fields into the HRIS and payroll systems.
7. The hiring manager manually schedules first-week meetings and shadowing
   sessions by emailing relevant team members individually.
8. Compliance training is assigned verbally by the manager; there is no
   automated tracking of completion.
9. Finance receives new-hire payroll setup information manually from HR,
   often by walking a paper form to the Finance office.

## Known Issues
- Frequent delays (5-10 business days) between offer acceptance and the
  employee having working IT access.
- Paper forms are sometimes lost, requiring re-collection of sensitive
  information (SSN, bank details) from the employee.
- No single system of record; HR, IT, and Finance each track onboarding
  status separately with no shared visibility.
- Compliance training completion is not tracked, creating audit risk.
